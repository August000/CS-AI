QS3.1 [option]:  For a general eliminate operation, which variables are unconditioned in the returned Factor? Which variables are conditioned?

The variables that are unconditioned in the returned factor are all of the previous unconditioned variables except for the eliminated one.
The variables that are conditioned in the returned factor are the same as the previous conditioned variables, as none are changed by elimination.



QS7.1: 

The main reason Pacman's belief at a square is updated by summing over all previous ghost positions: B'(newPos) = sum over oldPos of P(newPos | oldPos) * B(oldPos) after time passes. 
So a square gets lighter when a lot of likely old positions can move into it, or when the ghost's movement model prefers moving there. 
For the GoSouthAgent, the transition model gives extra probability to moving south, so lower squares become more likely over time. 
Squares get darker when fewer likely positions can reach them, when walls block movement into them, or when the ghost policy makes movement toward them less likely.
